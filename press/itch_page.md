## Description

u drew a lil guy in math class. forgot his legs.

notebook check is today, and doodle pages get ripped out. the only safe spot is haven: the back page no teacher has ever flipped to.

u have a rubber band, a pencil and a moral duty.

**how it works**

- drag back and let go. that's ur one shot.
- after that it's all bounce pads and walls.
- he stops moving, touches red pen (the spikes) or falls off the page? u draw the next guy. lil guy #2. lil guy #3. lil guy #48.

**what's in it**

- 9 pages, from a warm-up to THE LAST PAGE
- teal walls that keep all ur speed, paper walls that rip, pads that move, purple pads that only work once
- a secret page. look for something faint. a goat is involved
- UR GUY: pick his marker colour and his hat
- haven: every guy who makes it lives on the back page forever, with his number under him. lowest number wins the argument with ur friend

**controls**

mouse only. hold left click, drag away from where u wanna go, let go. any click skips the intro.

**credits**

made in Godot 4 by two friends. the font is Patrick Hand by Patrick Wagesreiter (SIL Open Font License). the sounds are made in code.
